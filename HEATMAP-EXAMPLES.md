## 🎯 Key Insights

### For Youth Films (College/Urban Youth)
- ❌ **Avoid**: March-April (Board Exams), May (IPL Season)
- ✅ **Best**: June-July (Post-exams), October-November
- 🟡 **Moderate**: December-January

### For Family Films
- ❌ **Avoid**: Working weekdays, mid-week releases
- ✅ **Best**: Major festivals (Diwali, Dussehra), Long weekends, School holidays
- 🟡 **Moderate**: Regular weekends

### For Kids Films
- ❌ **Avoid**: School exam periods (March, November)
- ✅ **Best**: Summer vacation (May-June), Diwali break, Christmas holidays
- 🟡 **Moderate**: Regular school days

### For Mass Films
- ❌ **Avoid**: Harvest season (working period)
- ✅ **Best**: Post-harvest, Sankranti, Pongal, Diwali
- 🟡 **Moderate**: Regular periods

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Multi-Audience Films**  
   If targeting multiple clusters, impact is averaged:
   - Youth (80%) + Family (-10%) = -45% average = 🟠 ORANGE

2. **Primary vs Secondary**  
   Primary audience clusters get more weight in scoring

3. **Critical Override**  
   Major exams/events can force RED even with good score:
   - Board exams + College audience = Always RED
   - IPL Final + Youth = Always RED

4. **Regional Sensitivity**  
   State-specific events only affect regional films:
   - Karnataka SSLC = Only impacts Kannada films
   - Tamil Nadu HSC = Only impacts Tamil films

5. **Hover for Details**  
   Always hover over heatmap dates to see:
   - Exact event names
   - Impact percentages
   - Competing movies
   - Detailed pros/cons
